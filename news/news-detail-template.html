Click for Change Project Funded by the Portuguese National Agency!

Free European Youth joins the international project as a partner, bringing young people together for digital participation, active citizenship, and social innovation.

July 15, 2026 4 min read Free European Youth e.V.
Cover photo area Upload your image and update: ../images/news/click-for-change-cover.jpg

Free European Youth e.V. is pleased to announce its participation as a partner in the Click for Change project, funded by the Portuguese National Agency. The international youth mobility will take place from 12–19 August 2026 in Braga, Portugal.

The project will bring together young people from different European countries to explore how digital tools can support meaningful participation, active citizenship, and positive change in local communities.

About the Project

Through non-formal education activities, group work, creative workshops, and intercultural exchange, participants will develop practical skills while sharing their own experiences and perspectives.

  • Digital participation and responsible online engagement
  • Active citizenship and democratic involvement
  • Social innovation and community-based solutions
  • Intercultural learning and European cooperation
“Click for Change will give young people the space and tools to transform ideas into concrete actions for their communities.”

Free European Youth’s Role

As a partner organisation, Free European Youth e.V. will support participant preparation, communication, dissemination, and follow-up activities. Our team will also contribute experience in youth participation, digital learning, and international cooperation.

What Happens Next?

Further information about participation, preparation meetings, and project results will be shared through our website and social media channels. Follow our updates to learn more about the activities and the impact created by the project.
